### 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the investment preferences of 40 investors, looking at who they are, what they invest in, why they invest, and how long they plan to hold their investments. The goal was to understand investors by their age, goals, risk considerations, and investment preferences, rather than treating every investor the same.

### 💡 Key Data Insights & Discoveries

* The market surveyed is made up of 25 men and 15 women, and regardless of gender, **Mutual Funds** are the clear top primary investment preference.
* **Established Adulthood (ages 26–30)** is the largest investor group, making up 53% (21 of 40 investors), and their primary investment influence is **Returns**.
* The younger group, **ages 21–25**, places much more weight on **Risk** than on returns — safety matters more to them.
* **Retirement Planning** is the leading savings objective at 58%. Combined with Healthcare, these two goals account for 37 of the 40 investors.
* The most common **investment duration** is **3 to 5 years**.
* Most investors — 32 out of 40 — expect returns between **20% and 30%**.

### 🛠️ Excel Skills & Dashboard Setup

To turn raw survey data from 40 investors into clear business insights, I built this dashboard using advanced Excel features and clean design practices:

* **Key Metric Tracking:** Wrote specific calculated fields to automatically surface the headline numbers — Total Investors (40), Gender Distribution (25 Male, 15 Female), Primary Investor Group (Established Adulthood, 53%), Primary Investment Preference (Mutual Funds), Primary Investment Influence (Returns, 65%), Primary Savings Objective (Retirement Plans, 58%), and Major Investment Duration (3–5 Years, 48%).

<img width="1884" height="126" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-02 051753" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bb32d696-9e9f-465a-9975-269d48896282" />

* **Smart Chart Design:** Built a set of clustered column charts to break the data down from multiple angles — investment type by gender, investment preference by age group, investment influence by age group, savings objective by investment duration, and mutual fund reasoning by expected returns — so each chart isolates one clear relationship instead of crowding everything into a single view.

* **Interactive Filters:** Added easy-to-use slicers (Gender, Age Group, Savings Objectives, Investment Driver) so anyone can click around and filter every chart on the dashboard instantly by investor segment.

## ✅ Strategic Recommendations

* **Build easy-to-understand mutual fund products around specific goals**, especially retirement and healthcare, since these two objectives cover the large majority of investors.
* **Target the 26–30 age group with growth- and returns-focused education and communication**, since they're the largest investor segment and returns are their main decision driver.
* **Communicate differently with the 21–25 age group** — lead with risk, diversification, and how the investment actually works, rather than leading with potential returns, since safety is their stronger concern.
* **Develop medium-term focused products and content**, built around a 3-to-5-year horizon, since that's the most common investment duration across the group.
